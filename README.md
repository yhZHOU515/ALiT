# ALiT
AI Platform for Librarian Lifelong Training (ALiT) is a three-year (2026-2029) workforce development project funded by the Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS).

<img width="376" height="176" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0d25dc7b-1f61-4e1b-8802-a9f4b536f1b7" />

ALiT positions generative AI as a simulation and reflection tool that supports knowledge sharing, experiential learning, ethical
decision-making, and reflective practice. ALiT is designed as an on-demand, flexible training environment, enabling
public librarians to practice professional service skills anytime and anywhere, without constraints of schedule, location, or
the availability of trainers or patrons.
